Tardy

A macOS menu bar app that makes meetings impossible to miss. Alerts escalate as a meeting gets closer, all the way to a flashing LATE counter, with one-click join.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

Universal (Apple silicon & Intel) · macOS 14+ · signed and notarized · updates itself

How it escalates

  1. Hours away WED16
  2. 30 min WED162:30-3:00pm
  3. 15 min WED1612m - Standup
  4. 5 min 🔴 4:59 - Standup (until 3:00pm)
  5. 1 min 🔴 0:42 - Standup  ·  beep beep beep
  6. Started ⚠ LATE 1:23

What it does

  • Sound and notifications A chime and a notification at 15 and 5 minutes, a live countdown, then beeps a minute out.
  • One-click join A big Join button appears as a meeting with a link gets close, or click any meeting in the list.
  • Work and Personal Mark each calendar Work or Personal. The dot beside the date tells you which kind is next.
  • Today at a glance Now, Starts in, Later today, with times and providers. Cmd+Shift+M opens it from anywhere.
  • Menu bar clock Optional seconds, AM/PM, 24-hour, day and date, plus a live clock at the top of the menu.
  • Light and private Wakes only when the display must change, reacts to calendar edits, and never sends your calendar anywhere.

Joins meetings on

Zoom · Microsoft Teams · Google Meet · Webex · GoTo Meeting · Amazon Chime · Slack Huddles · Whereby · Jitsi Meet · Discord · RingCentral Video · Zoho Meeting

Works with the Mac Calendar app. Add your iCloud, Google, Exchange or other calendars in Calendar > Settings > Accounts and Tardy watches them.
